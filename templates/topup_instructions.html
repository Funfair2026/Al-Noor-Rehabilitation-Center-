Top-up Instructions

Learn how to add money to your QR code balance

1️⃣

Find a Alnoor

Look for any Funfair alnoor staff with the "QR Code Top-up" sign. All alnoors can help you add money to your QR code.

2️⃣

Provide Details

Tell the alnoor staff your name (exactly as it appears on your QR code) and how much you want to add.

3️⃣

Pay by Card

Pay using your debit/credit card. The alnoor staff will process your top-up and confirm your new balance.

4️⃣

Enjoy

Your QR code balance is now updated! You can use it at any stall in the funfair.
